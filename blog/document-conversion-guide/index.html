Document conversion: choose the right format

Understand common conversions and what may change during conversion.

Practical guide · Updated November 2026

PDF to image

Useful for previews and sharing individual pages, but it can make text less searchable.

Images to PDF

Useful for combining scans or photos into a single document. Check orientation, order and resolution.

PDF to Word

Text extraction and layout conversion can vary. Inspect tables, columns, fonts and page breaks.

Preserve the source

Always keep the original file and review converted output before relying on it.
