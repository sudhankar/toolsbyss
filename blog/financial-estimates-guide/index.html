How to read financial estimates and calculators

Understand the difference between a formula output and a real-world financial outcome.

Practical guide · Updated November 2026

Nominal and real values

Inflation changes purchasing power. A future nominal amount may be worth less in today’s terms.

Interest and return are not identical

Loan interest, bank deposit rates and market returns have different risk and compounding characteristics.

Sensitivity matters

Small changes in tenure or rates can materially change long-term estimates. Explore a reasonable range.

No guarantee

Calculator outputs are illustrative only and should be verified against current official terms.
