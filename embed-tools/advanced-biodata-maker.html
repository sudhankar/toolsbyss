    About Advanced Biodata Maker

    This browser-based maker helps prepare a structured job or personal biodata for applications, office use and professional sharing. Add only information relevant to the intended employer or form. The simple ATS-style layout uses standard headings and selectable text in the browser print output; visual designs are provided for situations where a more styled document is appropriate.

    How to create your biodata

    1. Enter your name, contact information and the role you are applying for.
    2. Add a concise career objective and relevant skills.
    3. Add education, work experience, projects and certifications. Use the up/down controls to arrange repeated entries.
    4. Choose a design and theme colour, then review the live A4 preview.
    5. Use Print / Save as PDF and select A4 paper with default scale.
    6. Open the saved PDF once to check text, links, spelling and page breaks.

    Privacy and limitations

    Details and photos are processed in this browser. The optional draft save stores information in this browser's local storage on this device; turn it off on a shared computer or clear the saved draft when finished. JSON backups contain personal information and should be kept private. The keyword helper performs basic local word matching only. It does not use AI, connect to employers, verify certificates or guarantee ATS compatibility. Some employers and government applications require their own prescribed form; follow the official instructions where applicable.
