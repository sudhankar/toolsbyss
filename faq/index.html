Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account? You can read tool pages and guides without signing in; verified email sign-in is required to open interactive tools. Are accounts free? Firebase Authentication email/password is configured for free account creation, subject to Firebase plan limits. Can I use Google login? It can be enabled after configuring the Firebase Google provider. Are files uploaded? Some tools process data in the browser; check the page-specific privacy note. Does the PDF Editor change existing PDF text? It adds overlays and does not reliably rewrite embedded text. Can session history be exact after a crash? No; browser shutdown or loss of connectivity can prevent a final logout event, so the next login closes the earlier session approximately. Is AdSense approval guaranteed? No; Google makes its own review decision.
