Editorial Policy

ToolsBySS guides aim to be original, practical and transparent. Articles explain workflows, assumptions and limitations rather than promise outcomes. Tool pages should describe what the tool does, how to use it and what to verify. Financial topics are educational and should be checked against official, current sources. We correct material errors when identified; contact the site owner with the page URL and supporting details.
