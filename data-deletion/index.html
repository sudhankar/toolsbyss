Data Deletion & Account Requests

To request account deletion or removal of an activity record, contact the email configured in assets/js/master-config.js from the email address associated with the account. The site owner should verify the request, remove the Firebase Auth user and delete corresponding spreadsheet rows where required by the applicable retention policy. Some records may need to be retained for legal/security reasons. Do not email passwords or OTPs.
